The large typeset books, each as one PDF: A5 with mirror margins for printing (“A5 print”), A5 for 8″ tablets, and a Kindle edition that reads well on 6″ devices such as the Kindle Paperwhite. Thanks to [Veda Geetha Foundation, San Diego](https://www.vedageethafoundation.org/) and [Veda Circle, Los Angeles](http://veda-circle.org/), most are also available letter-sized in other scripts.
