<p class="mangala">ōṃ śrī gaṇēśāya namaḥ ।<br><br>
śrutismṛtipurāṇānām ālayaṃ karuṇālayam ।<br>
namāmi bhagavatpādaṃ śaṅkaraṃ lōkaśaṅkaram ॥<br><br>
sadāśivasamārambhāṃ śaṅkarācāryamadhyamām ।<br>
asmadācāryaparyantāṃ vandē guruparamparām ॥</p>

StotraSamhita is a collection of stotras, nāmāvalīs, pūjā-vidhānas and longer texts, carefully typeset and proofread, readable here in several scripts and downloadable as high-quality PDFs for printing and for devices such as the Kindle. Everything is typeset in LaTeX from source files kept on [GitHub](https://github.com/stotrasamhita); you are free to fork these repositories and typeset your own PDFs. Spotted an error? Every page links to its source file, and to a form for reporting the mistake.

Many of the stotras can also be heard, chanted verse by verse with the text on screen, on the [Stotra Samhita Vani](https://www.youtube.com/@stotrasamhita-vani) YouTube channel.

## Repositories

- [Stotra Sangrahah](https://github.com/stotrasamhita/stotra-sangrahah): the stotras, as single PDFs and as one book
- [Namavali Manjari](https://github.com/stotrasamhita/namavali-manjari): nāmāvalīs of 1000, 300 and 108 names
- [Puja Vidhanam](https://github.com/stotrasamhita/puja-vidhanam): pūjās, vratas and kathās
- [Vedamantra Book](https://github.com/stotrasamhita/vedamantra-book): veda mantras and sūktas, the Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa, Āraṇyaka and Kāṭhaka
- [Gita](https://github.com/stotrasamhita/gita): the Śrīmad Bhagavad Gītā
- [Adhyatma Ramayanam](https://github.com/stotrasamhita/AdhyatmaRamayanam): the Adhyātma Rāmāyaṇa, including the Uttara Kāṇḍa
- [Valmiki Ramayanam](https://github.com/stotrasamhita/ValmikiRamayanam) and [Mahabharatam](https://github.com/stotrasamhita/Mahabharatam)
- [Vani](https://github.com/stotrasamhita/vani): the chanted audio and videos
