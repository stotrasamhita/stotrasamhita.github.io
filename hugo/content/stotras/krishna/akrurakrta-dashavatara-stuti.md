<div class="verse-block-wrapper" id="akrurakrta-dashavatara-stuti-b0003"><div class="verse-block verse-justify"><div class="line">नमस्तेऽद्भुतसिंहाय साधुलोकभयापह।</div><div class="line" style="position:relative;">वामनाय नमस्तुभ्यं क्रान्तत्रिभुवनाय च॥३॥</div></div></div>
