<div class="verse-block-wrapper" id="shravana-mahatmyam-b0831"><div class="verse-block verse-justify"><div class="line">प्रसूत्यर्थं कनिष्ठोऽसौ तया सह गमिष्यति।</div><div class="line" style="position:relative;">तदा कार्यमिदं तात निर्विघ्नेन भविष्यति॥२१॥</div></div></div>
